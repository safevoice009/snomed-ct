# Change Proposal: Enterprise Clinical Suite Expansion

## Context
SICCE currently provides a 5-stage 3D camera flight, interactive Veryfi-standard optical prescription workbench with 3 authentic Indian hospital samples, SNOMED CT and AYUSH resolution, and ABDM FHIR R4 generation. To establish market dominance and commercial viability against Veryfi and Eka Care, we need 5 enterprise commercial and clinical extensions.

## Scope
1. **Interactive Enterprise ROI & Cost Calculator**: Real-time volume slider (5k - 500k Rx/mo) computing cost savings vs. Veryfi ($0.08 USD) in Indian Rupees (INR).
2. **Live Custom Image/PDF Upload & Mobile Camera in Workbench**: Drag-and-drop file upload allowing visiting developers/doctors to test optical extraction on their own custom prescription files.
3. **Clinical Drug-Drug Interaction (DDI) & Safety Alert Engine**: Automated checking for known interactions (e.g. Fluoroquinolones + Antacids, Paracetamol overdose) with real-time UI warning badges.
4. **Vernacular Patient Dosage Schedule Generator**: Automatic translation of prescription instructions into 5 Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali).
5. **One-Click Developer Export Hub**: Downloadable Postman Collection and OpenAPI 3.1 JSON for instant engineering integration.
