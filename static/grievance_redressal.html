Grievance Redressal Mechanism

In Compliance with Section 13 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

1. Statutory Redressal Process

Any Data Principal (Patient) or Data Fiduciary (Hospital/EMR Client) with grievances regarding personal health data processing, data rights, or information security may file a grievance through the designated channel.

Designated Grievance Officer

Name: Officer for Data Protection & Regulatory Compliance

Entity: SICCE Clinical Coding Engine India

Email: grievance@sicce.health

Jurisdiction: India (Nationwide)

Statutory Timeline: Acknowledgment within 24–48 hours; resolution within 7 working days.

2. Escalation to Data Protection Board of India (DPBI)

If a grievance is not resolved to the satisfaction of the Data Principal within the statutory timeframe, the Data Principal has the legal right under Section 13(4) of the DPDP Act 2023 to file an appeal before the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).
