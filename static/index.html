How it works

Four steps, one API call

POST a note or an image. Get back structured, coded, safety-checked clinical data.

STEP 01
Ingest & Sanitize
Text or prescription photo in. PHI scrubbed before anything leaves your control boundary.
STEP 02
Extract Entities
Symptoms, diagnoses, medications with dose & frequency — across English, Hindi and Hinglish.
STEP 03
Resolve & Safety-Check
Map to SNOMED CT concepts, resolve brands to generics, run CDSS interaction rules. Unresolved = flagged, never invented.
STEP 04
Emit Bundles
FHIR R4 consultation bundle out; NHCX-shaped insurance payload ready when you are.
